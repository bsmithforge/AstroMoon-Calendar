Frequently asked questions

What does the Moon sign on each day tell me?

It shows the Moon’s tropical zodiac sign at noon in your selected timezone. An ingress marks the time the Moon enters a new sign. Select a date for its noon position, timed events, and traditional interpretations, which are not scientific predictions.

Which zodiac does this calendar use?

The tropical zodiac uses twelve equal 30° signs, starting with Aries at the March equinox. Moon positions are calculated from Earth’s center using astronomy-engine and mapped to tropical signs rather than constellation boundaries.

Why can an event appear on a different date?

The same event can fall on different calendar days in different timezones. Choose your timezone under Your sky to use your local dates and times, including daylight saving changes. Eclipse entries mark global peaks and do not indicate local visibility.

How do I save or print my calendar?

Open Export, choose your dates and events, then download an ICS file for a one-time calendar import or a PDF for printing. Calendar PDFs include whole months, one per A4 page, up to 24 months. Data-table PDFs use your exact date range. ICS imports do not update automatically.

What does the hemisphere setting change?

Choose Northern or Southern under Your sky to flip the Moon illustrations. Event times, zodiac positions, and illumination percentages stay the same. The Moon’s actual tilt also depends on your location and observation time.
