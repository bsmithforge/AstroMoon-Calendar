Calendar Conventions & User Guide

What a Moon sign means in this calendar

The Moon’s instantaneous astrological and astronomical position in one of the 12 thirty-degree tropical ecliptic sectors (0° Aries to 360° Pisces). The Moon transits each sign every 2.25 to 2.5 days, reflecting traditional emotional and intuitive archetypes.

Which zodiac and calculation conventions are used

Tropical zodiac referenced to the true equinox of date, computing apparent geocentric ecliptic longitude (λ, 0°–360°). Zodiac sign ingresses (0° sector boundaries) are determined via numerical bisection search refined to 1 second or better.

How timezone selection affects dates

Celestial occurrences happen at a single universal instant (UTC). Selecting your observer timezone maps those universal timestamps to your local clock time and calendar day.

How to import the ICS and download the PDF

Click "Export (.ics / PDF)" to download an RFC 5545 calendar subscription file for Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, and Outlook, or compile a print-ready vector PDF ephemeris almanac.

What the hemisphere toggle changes

The physical illumination fraction is identical globally, but Northern Hemisphere observers view waxing crescents lit on the right, while Southern Hemisphere observers see them lit on the left. The toggle mirrors the visual lunar disk rendering accordingly.
